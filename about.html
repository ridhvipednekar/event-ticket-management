About Evently

We're on a mission to make campus events more accessible, simple and enjoyable for everyone.






Our Story

Evently was built by students, for students. We noticed how hard it was to keep track of campus events, get tickets and stay updated — so we created a simple, modern platform that brings everything together in one place.

From concerts and workshops to cultural fests and sports, Evently helps you never miss what matters.
